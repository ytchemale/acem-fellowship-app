Table 109-7: Energy Requirements for Defibrillation & Cardioversion

Rhythm Type of Shock Initial Dose Subsequent Doses
VF or Pulseless VT Defibrillation (Unsynchronized) 2 J/kg 4 J/kg (Max 10 J/kg or adult dose)
Unstable SVT or VT with pulse Synchronized Cardioversion 0.5 - 1.0 J/kg 2 J/kg

Table 109-2: Core Resuscitation Medications

Drug Route & Dose Key Remarks / Discriminators
Epinephrine IV/IO: 0.01 mg/kg (0.1 mL/kg of 1:10,000)
ETT: 0.1 mg/kg (0.1 mL/kg of 1:1000)		 ETT dose is 10x the IV dose! No evidence for high-dose parenteral epinephrine. Repeat every 3-5 min.
Amiodarone IV/IO: 5 mg/kg bolus May repeat up to 2 times for refractory VF/pVT (max 15 mg/kg/day). Contraindicated in long QT.
